## Production steps

1. (Optional) Put a voiceover at `audio/voice.mp3`, following the timing above.
2. Run `./render.sh` → `output/final.mp4`.
3. To edit text or numbers, change the `facts` array in `scene.html`. Scene timing is the `INTRO`/`FACT`/`OUTRO` constants there, and `starts` in `render.sh` must match.
